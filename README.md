# Hi, I’m Leo 👋  

A Robotics & AI Engineer with an M.Sc. in Robotics, Cognition, Intelligence from TUM. My background spans robot learning (RL/IL), robotics software development (Python, C++, ROS 2), and 3D perception/SLAM. I've worked on language-guided dexterous manipulation with Foundation Robotics, brain-inspired ML at BMW Research, and safe human-robot collaboration at TUM, contributing to a publication in IEEE Transactions on Robotics.

Beyond robotics, I enjoy exploring agentic AI through personal projects and hackathons. Born in Norway and raised in Germany, I'm excited to contribute to Europe's deep-tech ecosystem and build ambitious robotics and AI systems here.
